Builds the Sailor Saturn edition from two cartridges you own. Everything happens on this page: nothing is uploaded, and the page is not able to make network requests.

You need two clean Super Famicom dumps (a 512-byte copier header is fine):
  1. Bishoujo Senshi Sailor Moon S: Jougai Rantou!? — SHA-1 bc0e29ee383574443226695215496eb0d09aaa1c
  2. Bishoujo Senshi Sailor Moon SuperS: Zenin Sanka!! — SHA-1 1ada34177e7384612ae83464288f3860e4c4426e

Saturn, her voice and her stage come from SuperS. This page doesn't contain them: it rebuilds them from your copy.
